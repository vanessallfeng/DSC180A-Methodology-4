## Quarter 2 Project Reflection

**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

The most interesting topic has been using NLP to categorize and understand financial transaction descriptions. I think it's interesting how we can use methods like FastText to extract useful information from transaction memos and classify them into a set of categories that each team decided on.

**2. Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

I would be interested in investigating whether transaction categories can help capture different spending behaviors and improve credit risk predictions on a user to user basis. For example, we could examine whether patterns in recurring expenses, income, and debt payments provide useful information about financial stability.

**3. What is a potential change you'd make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**

One change I would make is to expand our labeled dataset and focus more on transactions that are ambiguous to classify. We could also compare different preprocessing methods and evaluate whether more detailed labeling improves model performance.

**4. What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?**

I would be interested in trying sentence embeddings or transformer-based NLP models and comparing their performance with TF-IDF and fastText.
